- Athyr (he/him): light, order, truth.
- Andros (he/him): darkness, freedom, and pleasure.
- Lumos (he/him): Sun and life. One of the original gods who created the world.
- Rehmir (she/her): Death and slumber. One of the original gods who created the world.
