#### Setting
For the past 3 years, a godly war has been raging on the continent of Iodor after a targeted attack on the highest order of the church of Athyr, killing the high priest. In response, the church of Athyr has waged war on the church of Andros.

Members of the party are under the care and protection of a rural and isolated Athyrian monastery located in the valley of the Cragcross mountain range. These reasons can vary, with some examples outlined below:
- Contracted to work for the monastery. Players can be hired to do labour and adventuring work at the monastery.
- Orphaned or abandoned. The player was taken in by travelling Athyrian missionaries at some point and placed in the monastery’s orphanage.
- Already in the monastery community.

Most reasons will work, just discuss with me!

#### Synopsis
In the past year, the monastery has begun to feel the effects of the holy war, taking in refugees and sending those who are capable to assist in the efforts of Athyrian church. The peace of this monastery is now threatened after centuries of isolated security. 

The players are called to duty by the Abbott of the monastery to take out an undead goblin camp under the control of Andros followers. This undead goblin camp has been terrorizing Athyrian settlements and refugee camps. High-level paladins are already on route to a separate situation, a town that is under attack, and the Abbott cannot afford to send any more out, in case of an attack on the monastery. 

Thus, your group has been brought together. Located on cursed Andros land a week away by horse, most communication and connection between players and their gods is severred, especially with Athyr. The group must navigate this with no external help in order to prevent anymore needless deaths from occurring.
