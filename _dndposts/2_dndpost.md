#### Setting
For the past 3 years, a godly war has been raging on the continent of Iodor after a targeted attack on the highest order of the church of Athyr, killing the high priest. In response, the church of Athyr has waged war on the church of Andros.

Members of the party are under the care and protection of a rural and isolated Athyrian monastery located in the valley of the Cragcross mountain range. These reasons can vary, with some examples outlined below:
- Contracted to work for the monastery. Players can be hired to do labour and adventuring work at the monastery.
- Orphaned or abandoned. The player was taken in by travelling Athyrian missionaries at some point and placed in the monastery’s orphanage.
- Born to parents within the monastery. Parents can either be permanent figures in the monastery or travelling Athyrian missionaries.

Most reasons will work, just discuss with me!
